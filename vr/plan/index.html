Phase 1: the lighthouse deal

The lighthouse goes on the Diocese of Fall River lot on East Britannia St, Raynham (owner: the Roman Catholic Bishop of Fall River, a corporation sole). Path: a friendly first talk with St. Ann's pastor > the bishop with his finance council and college of consultors > a ground lease or easement (preferred to a sale). The lot's assessed value is under the US bishops' canon 1292 minimum, so Holy See approval is not needed. Proposal PDF: in Josh's Fowling Pond folder.
