Concept rendering only, not a real institution or engineering design. Terrain: USGS 3DEP elevation. Ground imagery: USGS National Map orthoimagery (public domain), edited to show Fowling Pond Restoration Plan v6 (restored pond and wetlands, herring passage, King's Pond, regraded landfill/quarry, rail tunnel, relocated Thrasher St) approximately. Buildings, plants and animals are procedural concept models; sphinx heads: 3D scan of KAS38, cast of a sphinx with the features of Amenemhat, Royal Cast Collection, SMK - National Gallery of Denmark (public domain); the Propulsion hall and UFO room (through the doors in the banking hall's north wall) use procedural models and summarize public sources; the bank vault (lift in the banking hall, or Bank vault in the menu) and Josh's account board are concept displays, not a real account or balance; panoramas are AI-generated concept art and also light the 3D rooms; photo textures for grass, marble, limestone and wood: ambientCG (CC0). Zoo animals (llama, alpaca, zebra, deer, stag, horse): low-poly models by Quaternius (CC0), via Poly Pizza. Bridgewater detail area: USGS National Map orthoimagery and USGS 3DEP elevation (public domain); the launch pad and rocket are fictional. The flying saucer and its destinations are fictional procedural models; Dubai destination: building footprints, heights and building parts from Overture Maps Foundation (overturemaps.org, buildings theme, ODbL; OpenStreetMap and Microsoft footprints), roads, water and land use from Overture (OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). London destination: building footprints and heights from Overture Maps Foundation (overturemaps.org, buildings theme, ODbL), combining OpenStreetMap footprints and Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints (ODbL); roads, paths, trees and land use (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), ground elevation EU-DEM v1.1 (Copernicus, via OpenTopoData); the other cities are procedural impressions, not maps. Street names: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Desktop: WASD + mouse (drag to look), Q / E = turn left / right, H = home, F = fly, Space/Shift = up/down. Quest: left stick move, right stick turn, right trigger teleport, A/X or the Fly button on the menu = fly, press left stick or Y = menu.
