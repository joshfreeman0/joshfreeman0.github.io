Concept 1

Hot Yoga

A dedicated heated studio room, held at roughly 95 to 105°F with humidity, so classes can be taught as a hot practice.

Class types

  • Hot vinyasa
  • 26+2 style
  • Hot pilates

Design notes

  • Radiant heating to hold a steady studio temperature
  • Humidifiers to keep the room humid during class
  • Ventilation for fresh air without losing the heat
  • A non-slip floor that can handle moisture
  • Showers and a cold plunge nearby for after class
