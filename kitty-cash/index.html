Currency

Kitty Cash

The Love Kitten note, front and back.

Value

1 Kitty Cash = 48 carob of copper

That is 3.2 Q (quadrans), about 9.09 g of copper. One carob is 500/2640 g of copper and 15 carob make 1 Q.

At the Kitco copper ask on Nov 18, 2026 ($5.77 per lb), 9.09 g of copper is about $0.12 (11.6 cents). Copper moves daily, so this is a snapshot.

Count

Units: 10 meows = 1 prr, 10 prrs = 1 kitten, 10 kittens = 1 hiss, 10 hisses = 1 screech. So 100 meows = 1 kitten.
