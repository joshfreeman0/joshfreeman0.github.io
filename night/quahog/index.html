The Quahog Connection

A famous animated TV town in Rhode Island takes its name from a clam: the quahog.

The clam

The quahog is the hard-shell clam of the New England coast, Mercenaria mercenaria. Inside, its shell has a band of deep purple against white.

The beads

That purple and white shell is what true wampum beads are made from. Wampum jewelry is still made from it today, for example at Wampum World ↗.

The overlap

So the same clam names a cartoon town that millions of people know, and gives wampum its color. Most viewers have no idea the town's name is a real shell, let alone the shell behind wampum.

A marketing-hook idea

  • "The real quahog": the clam behind the famous name, in your hand as purple and white shell.
  • Short posts on what a quahog actually is, for people who only know the word from TV.
  • Keep it to the clam itself. No show names, characters or images, since those are trademarked.
