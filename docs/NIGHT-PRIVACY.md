# Day / night privacy rules

These rules apply to every page on this site, and especially to /night/.

- No personal contacts, relationships, partners or dates, family members, or friends' names.
- No locations, routines, home or property details, addresses, phone numbers or personal email addresses.
- No private-life details of any kind. If something is personal, it is left out. There is no login on this site, so nothing is "hidden" here; it is simply not published.
- Sponsor, affiliate and inquiry contact slots stay as "[To fill in]" placeholders until an approved business contact is chosen.
- No real affiliate links, reviews, ratings or endorsements until they are real and approved.
- Pages about bad actors or annoyances use generic archetypes only: no real people, companies or identifiable incidents.
- Visitor location is used only in the browser to work out sunrise and sunset. It is never stored or sent anywhere.
- Before publishing night-side changes, scan pages, scripts and styles for emails, phone numbers, personal names and place names.
