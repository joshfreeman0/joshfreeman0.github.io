Money

Units and play money concepts

Metal units

These units are tied to real metals, so their value follows the current gold, silver and copper prices.

  • c (gold) = 8 g of gold
  • xN (silver denarius) = 300/77 g of silver, about 3.896 g
  • Q (quadrans) = 15 carob of copper, where 1 carob = 500/2640 g, so 1 Q is about 2.841 g of copper
  • About 163 xN make 1 c, and about 157 Q make 1 xN.

Play money

Novelty play money, not legal tender. These notes are part of The Love Government concept.

Kitty Cash

The Love Kitten note. 1 kitten = 1 rawr (roar) = 100 meows = 10 prrs = 48 carob of copper = 3.2 Q, about 9.09 g of copper. 10 kittens = 1 hiss, and 100 kittens = 1 screech. About $0.12 per kitten at a copper price of $5.77 per lb on Nov 18, 2026.

Pup Money

1 pup = 9 carob of copper = 0.6 Q. 15 pups = 1 woof, and 25 woofs = 1 bark. About $0.02 per pup at the same copper price (Nov 18, 2026).

AH Money

1 AH = 5 pups = 45 carob of copper = 3 Q. About $0.11 per AH at the same copper price (Nov 18, 2026).

Mm Money

1 Mm = 45 pups = 405 carob of copper = 27 Q. About $0.98 per Mm at the same copper price (Nov 18, 2026).

Moo Money

1 moo = nine meows + one hiss = 1,009 meows (1 hiss = 1,000 meows = 10 kittens). About $1.17 per moo at a copper price of $5.77 per lb (Nov 18, 2026).

Honey Money

1 honey = 1 teaspoon of honey (about 5 mL or 7 g) = 45 carob of copper = 3 Q, the same as 1 AH. 100 drops = 1 honey, 10 drops = 1 dab, 3 honeys = 1 tablespoon, and 48 honeys = 1 cup. About $0.11 per honey at the same copper price (Nov 18, 2026).

Nut Money

1 nut = 15 carob of copper = 1 Q. 100 crumbs = 1 nut, 10 crumbs = 1 chip, 10 nuts = 1 handful, and 100 nuts = 1 sack. 3 nuts = 1 AH. About $0.04 per nut at the same copper price (Nov 18, 2026).

Dollar figures are approximate snapshots and change with the metal markets.
